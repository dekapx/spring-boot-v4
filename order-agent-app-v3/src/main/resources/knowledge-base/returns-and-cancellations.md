# Returns & Cancellations Policy

## Cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is PROCESSING.
- Once an order status becomes IN_TRANSIT or later, it can no longer be cancelled;
  the customer should instead wait for delivery and initiate a return.

## Returns
- Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund,
  provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.
- Electronics have a shorter 14-day return window due to warranty terms.
- Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items; otherwise the customer
  covers return shipping costs.

## Refunds
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after
  the returned item is received and inspected at the warehouse.

## Damaged or Missing Items
If an order arrives damaged or with items missing, the customer should report this
within 48 hours of delivery so a replacement or refund can be issued without needing
to return the original item.
