# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?
Every order gets a tracking number once it ships. Ask the order agent "where is my
order <order number>" and it will look up the live status, current location, and
estimated delivery date.

## What payment methods are accepted?
We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay / Google Pay at checkout.

## Can I change the item or quantity on an existing order?
Item and quantity changes are not supported after an order is placed. The order must
be cancelled (if still PROCESSING) and re-ordered with the correct item or quantity.

## How do I contact a human agent?
If the order agent cannot resolve an issue, customers can reach the support team by
email at support@example.com or by phone, available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm.

## Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to most countries. See the Shipping Policy document for details on
delivery times and customs fees for international orders.
