# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times
- Standard shipping: 5-9 business days from the order date.
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days from the order date.
- Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by weather, customs, or carrier delays.

## Carriers
We ship using DHL, FedEx, and An Post depending on the destination and package size.
Every order that has shipped includes a tracking number that can be looked up through
the order agent.

## Shipping Costs
- Orders over €75 qualify for free standard shipping.
- Orders under €75 are charged a flat €5.99 standard shipping fee.
- Express shipping is a flat €14.99 regardless of order value.

## Changing a Delivery Address
A delivery address can be changed only while the order status is PROCESSING or
IN_TRANSIT and the package has not yet reached the local delivery depot. Once an order
status is OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED, or CANCELLED, the delivery address can no longer
be changed through self-service, and the customer should contact the carrier directly
using the tracking number.

## International Orders
International orders may be subject to customs fees and import duties, which are the
responsibility of the customer and are not included in the order total.
